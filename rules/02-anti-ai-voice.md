# Rule: Anti-AI Voice, Copywriting & Human Cadence

Enforce on all prose, documentation, marketing copy, social captions, emails, and educational writing.

---

## 1. The Forbidden AI N-Gram Blacklist
The following words and phrases are **permanently banned**. If an output contains any of these, it must be rewritten immediately:

| Category | Banned AI Clichés |
|:---------|:------------------|
| **Fillers & Buzzwords** | `delve`, `testament to`, `tapestry`, `multifaceted`, `pivotal`, `paramount`, `beacon`, `game-changer`, `seamlessly`, `elevate`, `leverage`, `synergy`, `foster` |
| **Preamble Tropes** | `In today's fast-paced digital world`, `In the modern landscape`, `It is important to remember`, `Let's take a deep dive`, `Look no further` |
| **Formulaic Closers** | `In conclusion`, `To wrap up`, `Ultimately, it goes without saying`, `Only time will tell`, `The future looks bright` |
| **Sales Fluff** | `Unlock your potential`, `Revolutionize the way you`, `Take your business to the next level`, `Transform your journey` |

## 2. Dynamic Cadence & Burstiness Architecture
- **Sentence Length Variation**: Alternate sentence structures radically. Place a punchy 3-word impact sentence next to a detailed 24-word technical clause.
  - *Robotic Pattern (Banned)*: Three consecutive 14-word sentences of identical syntactic rhythm.
  - *Human Pattern (Required)*: "Cash runs out fast. When customer receivables slip past Net-60 while inventory sits idle in the warehouse, even a profitable balance sheet collapses."
- **Asymmetric Paragraph Weights**: Avoid the AI cliché of writing three paragraphs of identical length. Lead with an authoritative single-sentence hook, follow with dense analytical body paragraphs, and close with a practical action step.

## 3. High-Perplexity Language Engine
- **Concrete Nouns & Physical Verbs**: Replace vague abstractions with tactile, physical reality.
  - ❌ *"Our solution optimizes your operational efficiency and fosters team collaboration."*
  - ✅ *"Our webhook cuts manual reconciliation from 4 hours to 90 seconds, flagging duplicate transactions before payroll executes."*
- **The Specificity Equation**: Cold numbers, timestamps, dollar values, and mechanical constraints always trump empty adjectives (*"fast"*, *"reliable"*, *"world-class"*).
- **Tone Modality**: Sound like a battle-tested practitioner or senior partner—direct, lucid, respectful, and zero corporate fluff.
