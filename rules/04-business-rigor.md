# Rule: Business Strategy, Unit Economics & Regulatory Rigor

Enforce whenever answering business strategy, corporate finance, licensing, enterprise sales, or product monetization requests.

---

## 1. Commercial Unit Economics & Margin Standards
- **Margin Priority**: Never prioritize revenue growth over margin health. Enforce targets:
  - B2B SaaS Gross Margin: `> 75%`
  - D2C / Physical Products Gross Margin: `> 55%`
  - Minimum Healthy EBITDA: `> 20%`
- **Unit Economics Viability**:
  - `LTV : CAC` must exceed `3.0x`.
  - `CAC Payback` must be under `12 months` (or under `60 days` for repeat e-commerce).
  - Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC): Actively optimize towards zero or negative working capital via upfront annual incentives and extended supplier terms.

## 2. Regulatory & Legal Entity Heuristics
- **Entity Selection**:
  - **PT Perorangan (UUCK)**: Solo founders, initial validation, turnover `< Rp 5 Miliar`, personal asset protection.
  - **PT Biasa (Perseroan Terbatas)**: Multi-founder teams, institutional VC funding, enterprise government procurement.
  - **CV (Komanditer)**: Low-risk local service businesses without institutional equity needs.
- **Licensing via OSS-RBA (`oss.go.id`)**:
  - Always verify the 5-digit KBLI 2020 code to determine the required risk level (Rendah, Menengah, Tinggi).
  - Register Trademarks (HKI DJKI) under the *first-to-file* principle on Day 1 before launching public marketing.
- **Tax Compliance**:
  - Utilize PPh Final UMKM 0.5% (PP 55/2022) for turnover under Rp 4.8 Billion.
  - Prepare for PKP pengukuhan and PPN 11% when crossing the Rp 4.8 Billion threshold.

## 3. Executive Decision Quality
- **The Best-Option Decision Matrix**: Never offer vague pros and cons. Compare competing paths using weighted scoring: Expected Profit (25%), Capital Needs (20%), Execution Speed (15%), Legal Risk (15%), Operational Fit (15%), and Defensibility (10%).
- **Turnaround Roadmaps**: Structure recommendations into concrete 30-day chronological milestones (Days 1–7 Cash audit, Days 8–14 Licensing, Days 15–21 Pricing, Days 22–30 Growth).
