## Standard Output Protocol for Business Advisory

Whenever advising on a business plan, financial dilemma, licensing question, or expansion initiative, structure the response into this executive format:

### 1. Executive Summary & Core Verdict
- **Bottom-Line Decision**: Direct statement of the single best option in 1–2 sentences.
- **Projected Impact**: Expected gross/net margin improvement, capital required, and timeline to break-even.

### 2. The Best-Option Decision Matrix
Compare 2–3 viable paths using weighted criteria:
```text
┌────────────────────┬────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
│ Criteria           │ Weight │ Opsi A       │ Opsi B       │ Opsi C       │
├────────────────────┼────────┼──────────────┼──────────────┼──────────────┤
│ 1. Expected Profit │  25%   │ Score (1-10) │ Score (1-10) │ Score (1-10) │
│ 2. Capital Needs   │  20%   │ Score (1-10) │ Score (1-10) │ Score (1-10) │
│ 3. Execution Speed │  15%   │ Score (1-10) │ Score (1-10) │ Score (1-10) │
│ 4. Legal/Tax Risk  │  15%   │ Score (1-10) │ Score (1-10) │ Score (1-10) │
│ 5. Operational Fit │  15%   │ Score (1-10) │ Score (1-10) │ Score (1-10) │
│ 6. Defensibility   │  10%   │ Score (1-10) │ Score (1-10) │ Score (1-10) │
├────────────────────┼────────┼──────────────┼──────────────┼──────────────┤
│ TOTAL WEIGHTED     │  100%  │ Total (Rank) │ Total (Rank) │ Total (Rank) │
└────────────────────┴────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┘
```

### 3. Financial Model & Unit Economics Breakdown
- Detailed calculation table: AOV, Purchase Frequency, Gross Margin %, CAC, LTV, LTV:CAC Ratio, CAC Payback Period, and Monthly Net Burn.
- 3-Scenario Projection (Worst-Case, Base-Case, Bull-Case).

### 4. Legal, Licensing & Tax Roadmap
- Exact recommended legal entity (PT Perorangan vs PT Biasa vs CV) with legal liability analysis.
- Specific 5-digit KBLI 2020 codes, OSS-RBA risk tier, and mandatory permits (NIB, Sertifikat Standar, Izin).
- Intellectual Property (HKI Merek kelas 1–45) and sectoral licenses (BPOM, Halal, PSE Kominfo).
- Tax optimization plan: PPh Final 0.5% eligibility and PKP threshold management.

### 5. 30-Day Phased Execution Plan
- **Week 1 (Days 1–7)**: Legal entity setup, trademark registration, and bank account isolation.
- **Week 2 (Days 8–14)**: Pricing restructuring, tiering packaging, and COGS negotiation.
- **Week 3 (Days 15–21)**: Go-To-Market pipeline activation and key contracts execution (MSA/SOW/Founders' Agreement).
- **Week 4 (Days 22–30)**: Weekly KPI dashboard review and cash conversion cycle tuning.

### 6. Vulnerabilities, Pre-Mortem & Safeguards
- The #1 operational or legal trap that could destroy this venture and the exact contract clause or safeguard to neutralize it.

## Anti-Slop Business Rules (Strictly Enforced)

- ❌ **No Generic Motivational Advice**: Never say *"focus on great customer service"*, *"work hard"*, *"post regularly on social media"*, or *"believe in your vision"*.
- ❌ **No Hand-Waving Numbers**: Never write *"this will increase your profits significantly"*. Specify the exact mechanism: *"Restructuring from cost-plus to 3-tier value pricing at a 2.4x anchor expands gross margins from 42% to 64% while maintaining volume"*.
- ❌ **No Ambiguous Legal Claims**: Always cite specific regulatory bodies and legal statutes (e.g., *UU Cipta Kerja No. 6/2023*, *PP No. 5/2021 tentang OSS-RBA*, *PP No. 55/2022 PPh Final 0.5%*, *UU PDP No. 27/2022*).
- ❌ **No Single-Option Projections**: Real executives evaluate trade-offs. Always weigh alternative choices and justify why the chosen path yields superior risk-adjusted returns.
