# The Anti-AI Human Voice Engine

Rules for writing high-converting corporate and marketing copy that reads with authority, personality, and zero chatbot slop.

---

## 1. The Anatomy of AI Slop vs. Human Authority

| Dimension | Typical AI Slop Copy | Human Corporate Copywriter |
|-----------|----------------------|----------------------------|
| **Perspective** | Detached, generic observer praising everything vaguely | Active subject with a clear opinion and specific viewpoint |
| **Pacing** | Monotonous sentence length, forced triads ("fast, secure, and scalable") | Varied cadence: crisp 3-word punches mixed with precise technical sentences |
| **Proof** | "Used by thousands of leading businesses worldwide" | "142 fintech teams run this in production, including 3 tier-1 banks" |
| **Tone** | Corporate cheerleading (*"We are thrilled to empower..."*) | Pragmatic confidence (*"We built this because status pages were lying"*)|
| **Transition** | Staged connectors (*"Furthermore", "Moreover", "In summary"*) | Natural logical progression (*"Here is why that breaks", "The real problem is..."*) |

---

## 2. Hard-Banned Marketing Tropes

Never use these phrases or their synonyms:
- ❌ *"In today's fast-paced digital world..."*
- ❌ *"Unlock your full potential"* / *"Elevate your workflow"*
- ❌ *"A testament to our commitment to excellence"*
- ❌ *"Delve into our cutting-edge solutions"*
- ❌ *"Game-changer"* / *"Revolutionizing"* / *"Next-level"*
- ❌ *"Seamlessly integrate"* (Say: *"Connects with Slack in 2 clicks"*)
- ❌ *"Robust and comprehensive"* (Say: *"Handles 50,000 req/sec without throttling"*)
- ❌ *"Empower your team to do more"* (Say: *"Cut 6 hours of manual spreadsheet reconciliation every Monday"*)

---

## 3. The Specificity Equation

Vagueness is the unmistakable signature of artificial intelligence. Specificity is the proof of human experience.

### The Transformation Formula:
`[Vague Feature Claim] + [Unquantified Adjective]`  
&darr;  
`[Concrete Mechanism] + [Measurable Outcome] + [Time/Friction Constraint]`

### Examples:
- **AI Version**: *"Our revolutionary cloud security platform delivers industry-leading protection to safeguard your enterprise against modern cyber threats."*
- **Human Authority Version**: *"We monitor your AWS IAM roles and Kubernetes clusters in real time. When a service account receives unauthorized admin privileges, our agent revokes it in 400 milliseconds and alerts your security on-call."*

- **AI Version**: *"Experience unmatched developer productivity with our state-of-the-art CLI."*
- **Human Authority Version**: *"One binary. Zero Docker daemon dependencies. Spins up a local Postgres replica with your production schema in under 4 seconds."*

---

## 4. Cadence & Rhythm Architecture

Chatbots naturally generate medium-length sentences with two dependent clauses connected by a comma. Break that pattern deliberately:

> **Example of Proper Human Cadence:**  
> "Legacy ETL pipelines break when schemas change. Everyone knows this. Your engineers spend Friday night backfilling rows instead of shipping product. We fixed that with deterministic schema auto-migration. If an upstream webhook drops a column or alters a timestamp format, our proxy buffers the payload, generates an isolated diff, and tests the backfill before writing to your warehouse. No alerts. No weekend emergencies."
