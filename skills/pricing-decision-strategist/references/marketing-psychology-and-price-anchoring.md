# Marketing Psychology, Price Anchoring & Perceived Value Architecture

Strategic marketing and consumer psychology reference for engineering pricing power, overcoming sticker shock, and maximizing willingness to pay.

---

## 1. The Cost-Plus Trap vs. Value-Based Pricing

Most inexperienced founders calculate cost and add an arbitrary 30% margin. This **Cost-Plus Trap** causes two fatal errors:
1. **Underpricing Masterpieces**: If a formulation costs Rp 20.000 to make but solves a severe acne problem worth Rp 300.000 to the consumer, pricing it at Rp 35.000 destroys Rp 265.000 in perceived value and signals that the ingredients are cheap and ineffective.
2. **Overpricing Inefficiencies**: If high internal manufacturing waste makes your production cost Rp 80.000, customers do not care; they will not pay Rp 120.000 for a commodity worth only Rp 50.000 in their eyes.

```text
  [COST-PLUS PRICING] (Weak, Producer-Centric)
  Product ──► Direct Cost (COGS) ──► Add 30% Markup ──► Price ──► Convince Customer of Value

  [VALUE-BASED PRICING] (Dominant, Market-Centric)
  Target Customer ──► Identify Pain & Value ──► Determine Price Ceiling ──► Engineer Target COGS
```

## 2. Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Modeling

Determine the consumer willingness-to-pay corridor through 4 diagnostic questions:
1. **Too Cheap (Penetration Floor)**: At what price would you question the quality and refuse to buy?
2. **Bargain / Great Deal**: At what price is this a no-brainer high-value purchase?
3. **Expensive / Premium**: At what price does it feel pricey, but you would still consider buying?
4. **Too Expensive (Resistance Ceiling)**: At what price would you immediately walk away?

```text
  Percentage (%)
    100 │                                            / Too Expensive
        │   \ Too Cheap                             /
        │    \                                     /
     50 │     \               [OPTIMAL PRICE]     /
        │      \                     ▼           /
        │       \          ┌──────────────────┐ /
        │        \         │   SWEET SPOT     │/
      0 └─────────\────────┴──────────────────┴─────────────► Price (Rp)
                  Point of Marginal         Point of Marginal
                  Cheapness (PMC)           Expensiveness (PME)
```

## 3. Price Anchoring & Psychological Decoy Packaging

Consumers do not judge prices in an absolute vacuum; they judge them relative to the **first number presented (the anchor)**.

### The 3-Tier Asymmetrical Decoy Architecture:
| Package Tier | Name & Positioning | Strategic Role | Margin Profile |
|:-------------|:-------------------|:---------------|:---------------|
| **Tier 1: Starter / Entry** | "Single Pack / Mini" | Eliminates trial friction; establishes price floor. | 45% – 50% Margin |
| **Tier 2: The Hero (TARGET)** | "Best Value / 3-Pack Bundle" | **The Profit Machine (70% of volume)**. Positioned as only 40% more expensive than Tier 1 for 3x the quantity. | **65% – 72% Margin** |
| **Tier 3: The VIP Anchor** | "Ultimate Complete Regimen" | **The Decoy Anchor**. Loaded with exclusive bonuses, priced at 3x of Tier 2. Its primary job is making Tier 2 look like an irresistible bargain. | 75% – 82% Margin |

## 4. Price Ending Psychology: Charm vs. Prestige

The syntactic format of a price number sends an immediate subconscious signal regarding brand status:

### A. Charm & Fractional Pricing (Ending in `.900`, `.990`, or `.99`)
- **Mechanism**: Exploits the **Left-Digit Effect**. The brain processes numerals from left to right; Rp 199.000 is mentally categorized under the "100-thousand" bracket rather than "200-thousand".
- **Best Suited For**: FMCG retail, impulse purchases, e-commerce marketplace flash sales, high-volume consumer goods.
- **Perception**: "Discount, bargain, smart value purchase."

### B. Prestige & Rounded Pricing (Ending in `.000`)
- **Mechanism**: Clean, round numbers are processed effortlessly by the emotional brain (low cognitive load), signaling confidence, luxury, and premium status.
- **Best Suited For**: High-end skincare, artisanal F&B, luxury apparel, enterprise B2B consulting, fine dining.
- *Counter-Intuitive Truth*: Pricing a premium serum at Rp 349.000 makes it look like a cheap supermarket clearance item; pricing it at **Rp 350.000** commands luxury authority.
