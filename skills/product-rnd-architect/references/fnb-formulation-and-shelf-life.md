# F&B Product Formulation, Sensory Architecture & Shelf-Life Engineering

Master Research & Development (R&D) reference for developing shelf-stable, high-margin, commercially compliant food and beverage products.

---

## 1. Formulation Design & Sensory Architecture

Developing a commercial food or beverage product requires balancing **sensory excellence (taste/texture)**, **stability (anti-spoilage)**, and **unit cost economics**.

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ BASE CARRIER (70% - 90% of Volume/Weight)                             │
│ • Beverage: Reverse Osmosis (RO) water, milk base, botanical extracts │
│ • Food: Flour matrix, lipid/oil emulsion, starch gel, plant proteins   │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ FUNCTIONAL & TEXTURAL SYSTEM (2% - 10%)                                │
│ • Hydrocolloids / Stabilizers: Xanthan gum, pectin, guar gum, CMC      │
│ • Emulsifiers: Lecithin, mono- and diglycerides (prevents phase split) │
│ • Acidulants / pH Buffers: Citric acid, malic acid, sodium citrate     │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ SENSORY & FLAVOR SYSTEM (0.5% - 5%)                                    │
│ • Sweetness: Sucrose, stevia (Reb M/A), erythritol, allulose           │
│ • Flavor Delivery: Natural vs. Nature-Identical liquid essences        │
│ • Masking Agents: Blockers for bitter stevia or earthy pea protein     │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ PRESERVATION & STABILIZATION SYSTEM (< 1%)                             │
│ • Antimicrobials: Potassium sorbate, sodium benzoate (pH < 4.5)        │
│ • Antioxidants: Ascorbic acid (Vit C), tocopherols (anti-rancidity)   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Sensory Profile Radar (The 5 Core Dimensions):
1. **Initial Impact (Aroma & Top Notes)**: Volatile scent compounds detected upon opening and first sip/bite.
2. **Mouthfeel & Body**: Viscosity, creaminess, crispness, or carbonation bite. Measured in centipoise (cP) or texture analyzer force (N).
3. **Taste Balance**: Acid-to-Brix ratio ($^{\circ}\text{Bx} / \text{pH}$). In sweet beverages, target Brix 8°–12° with pH 3.2–3.8.
4. **Mid-Palate Continuity**: Flavor persistence without sudden hollow dips in taste profile.
5. **Clean Finish & Aftertaste**: Zero persistent metallic, oily, or unpalatable bitter lingering coating on tongue.

## 2. Shelf-Life Engineering: Water Activity ($a_w$) & Hurdle Technology

Food spoilage is governed primarily by **Water Activity ($a_w$)**, not total moisture content. $a_w$ represents the amount of free, unbound water available for microbial growth.

```text
┌───────────────┬───────────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────┐
│ $a_w$ Range   │ Microbial Risk                    │ Required Commercial Preservation      │
├───────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ > 0.95        │ High (Pseudomonas, Salmonella)    │ Retort sterilization (121°C), UHT,   │
│               │ (Fresh juices, milk, sauces)      │ or cold chain refrigeration (< 4°C)  │
├───────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 0.85 – 0.95   │ Medium (Staph aureus, molds)      │ Hot-fill (85°C–90°C), acidify pH<4.2,│
│               │ (Jams, syrups, soft bakery)       │ Potassium sorbate (max 1000 ppm)     │
├───────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 0.60 – 0.85   │ Low (Osmophilic yeasts, molds)    │ Modified Atmosphere Packaging (MAP), │
│               │ (Dried fruits, soft cookies)      │ oxygen scavengers, humectants        │
├───────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ < 0.60        │ Sterile environment (No microbes  │ Moisture-barrier foil packaging.     │
│               │ can multiply; crisps, milk powder)│ Shelf-stable 12–24 months ambient    │
└───────────────┴───────────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### The Hurdle Technology Equation:
Combine multiple sub-lethal hurdles rather than relying on a single heavy chemical preservative:
$$\text{Stability} = \text{Low pH } (< 4.2) + \text{Reduced } a_w + \text{Mild Thermal Pasteurization } (85^{\circ}\text{C}) + \text{Hermetic Barrier Packaging}$$

## 3. Accelerated Shelf Life Testing (ASLT)

Predicting 12-month ambient shelf life without waiting 365 days using the **Arrhenius Reaction Kinetic Model**:

$$Q_{10} = \frac{\text{Reaction rate at } (T + 10^{\circ}\text{C})}{\text{Reaction rate at } T} \approx 2.0 \text{ to } 2.5 \text{ for food quality loss}$$

### Standard Test Protocol:
- **Control Chamber**: Store at standard ambient ($25^{\circ}\text{C}, 60\% \text{ RH}$).
- **Accelerated Incubator**: Store at elevated stress ($40^{\circ}\text{C}, 75\% \text{ RH}$).
- *Time Equivalence*: Storing for **90 days at 40°C** approximately equals **360 days (12 months) at 25°C** for lipid oxidation and flavor degradation ($Q_{10} = 2.0$).
- *Test Checkpoints*: Days 0, 15, 30, 45, 60, 90. Measure: Total Plate Count (TPC), Yeast & Mold, Free Fatty Acids (FFA), Peroxide Value (PV), pH, and sensory triangular blind testing.

## 4. Packaging Barrier Engineering (OTR & WVTR)

Packaging is an active ingredient in product preservation. Selecting the wrong film ruins product quality within 30 days.

| Packaging Material | WVTR (Moisture Barrier) | OTR (Oxygen Barrier) | Best For |
|:-------------------|:------------------------|:---------------------|:---------|
| **Aluminum Foil Laminate (PET/ALU/PE)** | **0.00** (Impermeable) | **0.00** (Impermeable) | Premium coffee, chips, active botanicals (18–24 mo) |
| **Metallized PET (VMPET/PE)** | 0.5 – 1.5 | 1.0 – 2.5 | Snack foods, biscuits, tea powders (9–12 mo) |
| **Nylon Barrier (PA/PE)** | 5.0 – 10.0 | 20 – 40 | Vacuum frozen food, retort sauces (puncture resistant)|
| **Standard BOPP / LLDPE** | 4.0 – 6.0 | 1,200 – 2,000 (Leaky) | Short shelf-life bakery, dry noodles (< 3 mo) |

## 5. Indonesian Regulatory Roadmap: P-IRT vs. BPOM MD vs. Halal

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ P-IRT (Pangan Industri Rumah Tangga) - Dinas Kesehatan                 │
│ • Batasan: Skala mikro/kecil, lokasi tempat tinggal/dapur UMKM         │
│ • Jenis Produk: Hanya pangan kering/awet dengan $a_w < 0.85$           │
│   (Kue kering, keripik, abon, bubuk kopi, selai kemasan).             │
│ • DILARANG P-IRT: Frozen food, susu/dairy, daging olahan, pangan steril│
│   komersial, pangan kaleng, air minum dalam kemasan (AMDK).            │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ BPOM MD (Pangan Olahan Dalam Negeri) - BPOM RI                         │
│ • Wajib jika: Pabrik terpisah dari rumah, ada mesin otomatis,          │
│   produk berisiko tinggi (frozen, minuman RTD cair, saus retort).      │
│ • Prasyarat: PSB (Pemeriksaan Sarana Bangunan) layout higienis (CPPOB).│
│ • Uji Lab Wajib: Cemaran mikroba (Salmonella, E. coli), logam berat    │
│   (Pb, Cd, As, Hg), dan Informasi Nilai Gizi (AKG format resmi).       │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ SERTIFIKASI HALAL (BPJPH & LPPOM MUI)                                  │
│ • Wajib bertahap untuk semua F&B di Indonesia (UU JPH 33/2014).        │
│ • Dokumen: SJPH (Sistem Jaminan Produk Halal), matriks bahan baku,     │
│   sertifikat halal seluruh supplier bahan baku (Zero Haram Risk).      │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
