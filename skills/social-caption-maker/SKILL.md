## The 4 Laws of Viral Social Captions

### 1. Win the 80-Character Cutoff (The Hook)
The first 1–2 lines determine 90% of your post's reach. Never start with greetings (*"Halo teman-teman", "Hey guys!"*) or generic intros (*"In this video I want to share..."*).
- Start immediately with the contrarian fact, curiosity gap, or relatable truth.
- Force the user to click **"...more"** to signal high engagement to the algorithm.

### 2. Social SEO Keyword Embedding (Search Ranking)
Social media platforms are now modern search engines:
- Embed the exact natural search query into the first 2 sentences (e.g. *"cara bikin website portofolio modern"*, *"tips belajar UI UX pemula"*, *"best tools for frontend developers"*).
- Use 3–5 hyper-targeted niche hashtags instead of 30 spam tags (`#fyp #viral` are useless for real search ranking).

### 3. Anti-Cringe & Gen Z Authenticity Filter
- ❌ **No Emoji Overdose**: Never start every sentence with `✨🚀🔥🎉💡`. Use at most 1–3 clean emojis (📌, ⚡, ☕, 👁️) as functional markers.
- ❌ **No Forced Slang**: Never force internet slang into contexts where it feels artificial. Modern audiences appreciate clean, effortless, conversational authenticity.
- ❌ **No Preachy AI Closers**: Replace *"Don't forget to like, comment, and subscribe! 👇"* with organic, conversational prompts (*"Save ini buat contekan pas lo redesign nanti"* or *"Lo tim yang mana: A atau B?"*).

### 4. Optimize for the Highest-Weight Algorithmic Action
Tailor the ending trigger based on your post type:
- **Educational / Tutorial** &rarr; **Save Trigger** (*"Save postingan ini biar gampang dicari pas lo butuh nanti 📌"*).
- **Contrarian / Opinion** &rarr; **Comment Trigger** (*"Menurut lo wajar gak atau overrated? Tulis pendapat lo di bawah 👇"*).
- **Relatable / POV** &rarr; **Share Trigger** (*"Kirim ini ke temen lo yang tiap hari lembur cuma gara-gara layout CSS"*).
