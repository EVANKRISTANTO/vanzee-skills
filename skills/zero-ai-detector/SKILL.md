## The 5 Laws of 0% AI Detection

### 1. Maximize Burstiness (Radical Sentence Variance)
AI detectors flag paragraphs where sentences have uniform lengths (15–22 words).
- **Rule**: In every paragraph, force at least one ultra-short sentence (< 6 words) right before or after a complex compound sentence (> 25 words).
- Mix sentence types: declarative, rhetorical questions, parenthetical observations `(like this)`, and direct fragments.

### 2. Elevate Perplexity with Concrete Domain Specificity
Replace generic, predictable words with domain nouns and active physical verbs.
- ❌ *"The technology provides innovative solutions for business scalability."* (Low perplexity &rarr; 99% AI score).
- ✅ *"The proxy batches Postgres writes every 250 milliseconds, cutting connection pool exhaustion during peak traffic."* (High perplexity &rarr; 0% AI score).

### 3. Absolute Ban on AI N-Grams
Zero tolerance for detectable AI staples:
- Banned: *delve, testament, pivotal, beacon, multifaceted, foster, tapestry, navigate the complexities, in conclusion, furthermore, moreover, seamlessly, elevate, unlock, unleash*.

### 4. Asymmetrical Structural Cadence
- Delete the standard robotic template: *[Staged Opening] &rarr; [3 Equal Body Points with bold labels] &rarr; [Optimistic Summary Closer]*.
- Use uneven, natural paragraph weights: a meaty 6-sentence technical breakdown followed by a 1-sentence reality check.

### 5. Intentional Human Voice Markers
Inject rhetorical devices that language models systematically avoid:
- Occasional colloquial transitions: *"Here's the problem:", "Fair enough.", "That didn't happen."*
- Pragmatic trade-offs and personal voice: *"In practice, this sounds better on paper than it actually works in production."*

## Transformation Workflow

When given text to humanize:
1. Scan for and strip all banned AI vocabulary and connector words.
2. Break uniform sentences into asymmetric bursty cadences.
3. Replace abstract claims with concrete nouns and real-world mechanisms.
4. Verify the output reads naturally when spoken out loud.
